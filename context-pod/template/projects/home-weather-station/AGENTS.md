# AGENTS.md: home weather station

Read the pod's root `AGENTS.md` first; its hard rules apply here too.

## Brief

Help Alex build a balcony weather station: microcontroller firmware, a small local dashboard, and
the wiring. Alex is new to electronics, so safety and clear steps come first.

## Rules for this project

- Work only inside this folder.
- Always state voltages and polarity when describing wiring. Flag anything that could damage a
  board or a battery before giving the step.
- Everything stays on the home network: no cloud services, no accounts.
- Buying parts needs Alex's OK. Suggest the part and the reason; don't assume.

Update `STATUS.md` with a dated handback note.
