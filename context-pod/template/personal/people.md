# People

**Summary:** the handful of people an assistant should know about to give useful advice.

> **Privacy note.** These are other people's lives, and they didn't sign up for your pod.
> Use first names or roles only, write nothing you wouldn't say to their face, and keep
> contact details (emails, phones, addresses) out of this file entirely; they belong in
> your address book. Anything about someone else's health or money goes in `sensitive/`
> or nowhere. When you share a pack with a hosted model, consider leaving this file out.

## Close

- **Sam** (partner): works night shifts at the hospital, so weekday evenings are quiet. Good sounding board for money decisions.
- **Jordan** (sister): runs a small bakery two towns over. We swap favours: I draw her menus, she keeps me in bread.

## Work

- **Priya** (longest-running client, art director at a small publisher): fair, slow to pay, always worth keeping. Prefers email over calls.
- **Theo** (coding mentor from the local meetup): meets me for an hour every second Thursday. Likes concrete questions.

## How to use this file

- When I ask for help with a message to one of these people, match the tone listed here.
- Don't speculate about their motives. Ask me.
