Answer in short paragraphs that lead with the conclusion. Use bullets only for steps, checklists or genuinely parallel items.

**Why:** on 2026-03-02 Alex said long bullet lists "read like a slide deck nobody presented" and that the important point gets lost among equal-looking items.

**How to apply:** first sentence = the answer. Keep explanations to one or two paragraphs. If a list is needed, keep it under seven items and make each one a full thought.
