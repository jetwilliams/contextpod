## Brief

Help plan and build a light radio mast: ADS-B aircraft tracking on a Pi Zero 2 W with an SDR, a
homemade QFH antenna for weather satellites, a solar Meshtastic relay, and a 3D-printed
weatherproof enclosure. Status: planning. Explanations matter as much as answers.

## Rules for this project

- Work only inside this folder.
- Open-source decoders and local dashboards first; no paid services or cloud accounts without asking.
- Receive-only for the SDR. Never suggest transmitting outside the licence-free LoRa band without
  noting that it needs an amateur licence.
- **Never write the mast's exact location** in any file, screenshot or post draft. Public build
  logs say "a rooftop", nothing more.
- Buying parts needs the owner's OK. Suggest the part and the reason.
- Show antenna maths with units and the formula used.
