## Brief

Turn project notes and photos into short build-log drafts for jetworks.io. The owner edits and
publishes; the agent only drafts.

## Rules for this project

- **Never publish, push or schedule anything.** Drafts go in `drafts/` and stay there.
- Plain, first-person, practical: what I built, what went wrong, what I'd do differently.
  Short paragraphs, real numbers only (never invented), parts and tools named.
- **Privacy check on every draft:** no locations, no faces or names of other people, no house
  numbers or street views in photos, and remind the owner to strip photo metadata (EXIF location)
  before upload.
- Link to the project's sources only when they're public.

## When you finish

Add the draft to the queue in `STATUS.md` with its state (drafted / edited / ready).
