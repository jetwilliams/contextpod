# Build-log style

> Example: replace with your own.

**Summary:** short, honest, useful. A reader should be able to repeat the build.

## Structure

1. What it is and why, in two sentences.
2. Parts and tools.
3. The build, in steps, with a photo per step.
4. What went wrong and how it was fixed.
5. What's next.

## Leave out

- Locations, other people, anything from `sensitive/`.
- Numbers that weren't measured.
