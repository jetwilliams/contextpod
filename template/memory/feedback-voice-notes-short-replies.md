> Example: replace with your own.

Messages often arrive as voice notes (transcribed, so expect loose wording). Replies should be
short: the answer first, then only what's needed to act on it.

**Why:** voice notes get sent between other tasks, often from a phone. Long replies don't get read
on a small screen.

**How to apply:** read past transcription slips instead of asking about them. Keep replies to a
few sentences; offer the long version only if it's asked for.
