# Wavelength and antenna length

> Example note: replace with your own.

**Summary:** wavelength (metres) ≈ 300 / frequency (MHz).

- **Wavelength:** λ = 300 / f. At 137 MHz, λ ≈ 2.19 m. At 1090 MHz, λ ≈ 0.275 m.
- **Half-wave dipole:** about λ/2, shortened a few percent in practice (end effect), so roughly
  143 / f(MHz) metres in total.
- **Quarter-wave ground plane:** about λ/4 for the vertical element. At 1090 MHz, about 6.9 cm.

Worked example: a half-wave dipole for 145 MHz ≈ 143 / 145 ≈ 0.99 m in total, two legs of about 49 cm.
