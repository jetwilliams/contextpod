# Licence basics

> Example note: replace with your own. Rules vary by country; check your regulator.

**Summary:** in most countries, receiving is unrestricted for hobby use but transmitting on
amateur bands needs a licence.

- **Receiving** (SDR, aircraft, weather satellites): generally allowed; some places restrict
  sharing or acting on what you hear.
- **Transmitting on amateur bands:** needs a licence and a call sign; power and bands depend on the
  licence class.
- **Licence-free bands** (e.g. LoRa ISM bands used by Meshtastic): no licence, but strict limits on
  power and duty cycle.
