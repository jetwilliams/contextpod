# CIA triad

> Example note: replace with your own.

**Summary:** most security controls protect one of three things.

- **Confidentiality:** only the right people can read it. Controls: encryption, access control.
- **Integrity:** it hasn't been changed without anyone noticing. Controls: hashing, digital signatures.
- **Availability:** it's there when needed. Controls: backups, redundancy, DDoS protection.

**Quick check:** a ransomware attack mostly hits *availability*; a leaked database hits
*confidentiality*; a tampered download hits *integrity*.
